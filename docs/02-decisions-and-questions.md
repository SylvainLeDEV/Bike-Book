| D-69 | 2026-10-27 | **Safe migrations.** The app applies pending migrations with foreign keys **off** (set before drizzle's transaction, where the pragma would be ignored), then runs `PRAGMA foreign_key_check`: any dangling reference stops the app with an error naming the tables (the migrations are already committed: restore the backup taken before the upgrade), else foreign keys are turned back on. Rules for writing migrations: never edit, rename or delete a migration once released (drizzle compares only each migration's `when` with the last one applied, not its content, so an edited migration is silently skipped on existing databases); prefer additive changes (new table, new nullable or defaulted column, new index); a table rebuild generated by drizzle-kit is allowed but read before committing; data fixes go in their own migration | drizzle-kit changes a column by rebuilding the table (`__new_x`, copy, `DROP TABLE x`, rename). The migration's own `PRAGMA foreign_keys=OFF` is a no-op inside the transaction (**Verified 2026-10-27** in drizzle-orm's SQLite migrator and by a test). Migrations 0000 to 0008 contain no rebuild |
| D-70 | 2026-10-27 | **Editing and deleting rides and maintenance.** Every entry of Activité and of the bike page's history opens its sheet filled (a chevron marks it; the dashboard's recent activity stays read-only). A ride reopens the quick entry ("Modifier la sortie"): bike, date, type, durations, distance, elevation, conditions, place, start time (noon is shown as no time, D-61) and notes; an edited ride keeps its type when moved to another bike of the workspace; the fields the sheet does not show (`title`, `descent_distance_m`, `elevation_loss_m`) stay as they are, and `moving_time_s` is dropped when "Durée" changes, since it would otherwise still be shown and counted (D-13). A maintenance event is edited on its bike's page (Activité opens `/bikes/[id]?event=<id>`, where its parts and rules are): header and lines are rewritten (lines other than replacements deleted and inserted again), `created_at` and the bike never change, parts removed since stay offered as "(déposée)". Its replacements are listed read-only and fix its date (the parts were fitted and removed at its instant); such an event cannot be deleted, a replacement being undone from its own toast only. "Supprimer…" asks for a confirmation in the sheet's footer and is a hard delete without "Annuler" (it only fixes a mistake, [03](03-data-model.md#conventions)); rides imported later (`source` other than `manual`) cannot be deleted | Mistakes happen when typing fast (principle 1), and "Annuler" only covers the few seconds after saving. A confirmation is simpler than an undo that would have to recreate the row |
